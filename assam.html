Assam – The Land of Tea & Wildlife

Assam is a northeastern state known for its lush tea gardens, mighty Brahmaputra River, wildlife sanctuaries, and rich cultural heritage. It is a paradise for nature lovers, history enthusiasts, and cultural travelers.

Best time: Oct – Apr
Ideal stay: 4 – 6 days
Famous for: Tea gardens, Wildlife, Brahmaputra
Major cities: Guwahati, Kaziranga, Tezpur, Majuli

Why visit Assam?

Assam offers breathtaking landscapes, unique wildlife, and rich traditions. Explore Kaziranga National Park, cruise along the Brahmaputra River, visit tea estates, experience Bihu festivals, and discover the culture of the Ahom dynasty.

Top attractions

Kaziranga National Park

Majuli Island

Kamakhya Temple

Food & Experiences

  • Assamese cuisine – Fish tenga, Pitha, Assam tea.
  • River cruises on the Brahmaputra.
  • Wildlife safaris at Kaziranga and Manas.
  • Cultural festivals – Bihu, Ambubachi Mela.

Good to know

Best time: October – April for pleasant weather and wildlife sightings.

Activities: Safari tours, river cruises, tea garden visits, cultural festivals.
