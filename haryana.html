Haryana – The Land of Culture & Heritage

Haryana, known as the "Abode of God", is a state rich in history, mythology, and vibrant rural traditions. From sacred sites like Kurukshetra to modern hubs like Gurugram, Haryana offers a perfect mix of spirituality, heritage, and progress.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 2 – 4 days
Famous for: Spirituality, Heritage, Fairs
Major Cities: Gurugram, Faridabad, Kurukshetra

Why visit Haryana?

Haryana is deeply rooted in Indian history and mythology. It is believed to be the land where the epic battle of Mahabharata was fought, and where Lord Krishna delivered the Bhagavad Gita in Kurukshetra. At the same time, it has developed rapidly with modern cities and infrastructure, making it both traditional and contemporary.

Top attractions

Brahma Sarovar, Kurukshetra

Sultanpur National Park

Sheetla Mata Mandir, Gurugram

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather.

Fairs & Festivals: Surajkund Mela, Mango Mela, Teej Festival.

Food: Haryana’s rustic cuisine includes Bajra Khichdi, Kadhi, and fresh dairy delights like Lassi & Ghee.

Shopping: Handlooms, handicrafts, and rural art at Surajkund fair.
