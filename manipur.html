Manipur – Jewel of Northeast India

Manipur is a scenic northeastern state known for its rich culture, classical dance forms, serene lakes, and rolling hills. It offers a unique blend of tradition, natural beauty, and adventure.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Loktak Lake, Dance & Culture
Major cities: Imphal, Moirang, Ukhrul

Why visit Manipur?

Manipur offers a unique mix of scenic landscapes, cultural richness, and adventure. Explore Loktak Lake’s floating islands, Kangla Fort, classical dance performances, and hill treks in Ukhrul and Churachandpur.

Top attractions

Loktak Lake

Imphal

Kangla Fort

Food & Experiences

  • Manipuri cuisine – Eromba, Singju, local bamboo shoots dishes.
  • Classical Manipuri dance performances.
  • Boat rides and photography at Loktak Lake.
  • Hill treks and tribal village visits.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.

Activities: Lake tours, trekking, cultural experiences, sightseeing.
