Karnataka – Heritage, Nature, and Cities

Karnataka offers a mix of historic sites, lush landscapes, hill stations, and vibrant cities. From the palaces of Mysore to the tech hubs of Bangalore and the coffee plantations of Coorg, there’s something for every traveler.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 7 – 10 days
Famous for: Heritage, Hill stations, Wildlife
Major cities: Bangalore, Mysore, Mangalore, Hubli

Why visit Karnataka?

Karnataka blends history, natural beauty, and culture. Explore the palaces and temples in Mysore, trek in Coorg and Chikmagalur, enjoy beaches at Gokarna, and visit Hampi's UNESCO World Heritage ruins.

Top attractions

Mysore Palace

Hampi Ruins

Coorg Hill Station

Food & Experiences

  • South Karnataka cuisine – Bisi Bele Bath, Mysore Pak, Ragi Mudde.
  • Temple tours and heritage walks.
  • Coffee plantations and trekking in Coorg & Chikmagalur.
  • Beaches and water sports in Gokarna.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Activities: Heritage tours, hill station trekking, coffee plantation visits, wildlife exploration.
