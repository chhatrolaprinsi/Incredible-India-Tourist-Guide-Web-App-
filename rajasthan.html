Rajasthan – Land of Kings

Rajasthan is known for its royal palaces, historic forts, desert landscapes, and colorful culture. From Jaipur’s bustling markets to Jaisalmer’s golden sand dunes, Rajasthan offers a blend of history, adventure, and vibrant traditions.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Palaces, Forts, Desert
Major cities: Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer

Why visit Rajasthan?

Rajasthan is India’s most iconic desert state, with centuries-old palaces, forts, and temples. Explore camel safaris in the Thar Desert, stroll through colorful markets, and enjoy Rajasthani cuisine and folk music. The state offers history, adventure, and unforgettable cultural experiences.

Top attractions

Amber Fort, Jaipur

City Palace, Udaipur

Jaisalmer Fort

Food & Experiences

  • Dal Baati Churma – traditional Rajasthani meal.
  • Laal Maas – spicy mutton curry.
  • Ghevar – sweet dessert, especially during festivals.
  • Cultural experiences – folk dances, camel safaris, desert camping.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather.

Activities: Desert safaris, heritage tours, city sightseeing, cultural festivals.
