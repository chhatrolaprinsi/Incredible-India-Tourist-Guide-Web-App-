Punjab – The Land of Five Rivers

Known as the heart of India’s Sikh culture, Punjab is a land of vibrant festivals, golden fields, soulful music, and mouth-watering cuisine. With its rich history and warm hospitality, Punjab is a must-visit destination for travelers.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 4 days
Famous for: Golden Temple, Bhangra, Food
Airport/Rail: ATQ / LDH / Amritsar Jn.

Why visit Punjab?

Punjab is not just India’s breadbasket, it is a cultural powerhouse. From spiritual experiences at the Golden Temple to lively celebrations of Baisakhi, Punjab offers a unique blend of history, tradition, and modern vibrancy.

Top attractions

Golden Temple, Amritsar

Wagah Border Ceremony

Jallianwala Bagh

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather.

Food: Must-try dishes include Makki di Roti with Sarson da Saag, Amritsari Kulcha, and Lassi.

Festivals: Baisakhi in April, Lohri in January, Hola Mohalla.

Shopping: Phulkari embroidery, Punjabi juttis, handicrafts.

Want to explore Punjab?
From spiritual journeys to food trails, Punjab has something for everyone.
