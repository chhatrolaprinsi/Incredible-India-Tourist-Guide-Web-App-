Lakshadweep – Tropical Islands & Coral Reefs

Lakshadweep is India’s archipelago of tropical islands, famous for crystal-clear waters, coral reefs, and sandy beaches. Perfect for scuba diving, snorkeling, and serene island getaways.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Beaches, Coral reefs, Water sports
Major islands: Agatti, Bangaram, Kadmat, Minicoy

Why visit Lakshadweep?

Lakshadweep offers pristine beaches, turquoise waters, and lush coconut palms. Snorkeling, scuba diving, and lagoon exploration are highlights. The islands are peaceful, with minimal crowds, making it perfect for relaxation and nature lovers.

Top attractions

Bangaram Island

Agatti Island

Minicoy Island

Food & Experiences

  • Seafood specialties – fish curry, mas huni, coconut-based dishes.
  • Water sports – snorkeling, scuba diving, kayaking.
  • Relax on untouched beaches and enjoy sunset views.
  • Visit coconut plantations and local villages.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for sunny, calm weather.

Activities: Diving, snorkeling, island hopping, beach relaxation.
