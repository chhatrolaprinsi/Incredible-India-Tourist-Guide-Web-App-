Chhattisgarh – Land of Waterfalls and Forests

Chhattisgarh, in central India, is known for its dense forests, tribal culture, waterfalls, and wildlife sanctuaries. It’s perfect for nature lovers and cultural explorers.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Waterfalls, Forests, Tribal Culture
Major cities: Raipur, Jagdalpur, Bilaspur

Why visit Chhattisgarh?

Chhattisgarh offers stunning natural beauty, tribal heritage, and waterfalls. Explore Chitrakote Falls, Tirathgarh Falls, Kanger Valley National Park, and experience local culture and crafts.

Top attractions

Chitrakote Falls

Tirathgarh Falls

Kanger Valley National Park

Food & Experiences

  • Traditional Chhattisgarhi dishes – Chana Samosa, Faraa, Muthiya.
  • Visit tribal villages to experience local crafts and lifestyle.
  • Trekking, wildlife spotting, and waterfall sightseeing.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Activities: Trekking, waterfalls, wildlife safaris, tribal culture tours.
