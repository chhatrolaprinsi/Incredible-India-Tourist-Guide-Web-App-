Himachal Pradesh – The Land of Hills

Himachal Pradesh is a paradise for nature lovers, adventure seekers, and spiritual travelers. With its snow-capped mountains, lush valleys, rivers, and hill stations, Himachal Pradesh offers breathtaking landscapes and peaceful retreats.

Best time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Hill Stations, Adventure, Temples
Major Cities: Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala

Why visit Himachal Pradesh?

Himachal Pradesh is famous for its scenic beauty and adventure activities. Whether you want to trek in the Himalayas, explore monasteries in Dharamshala, ski in Solang Valley, or simply relax in apple orchards, Himachal has it all.

Top attractions

Shimla

Manali

Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj

Good to know

Best time: March – June for clear weather, Sept – Nov for autumn colors.

Activities: Trekking, paragliding, skiing, river rafting.

Food: Try local Himachali dishes like Chha Gosht, Dham, Babru, Siddu.

Shopping: Woolens, handicrafts, tea, Himachali caps.
