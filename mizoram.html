Mizoram – Land of the Hill People

Mizoram is a picturesque northeastern state known for its rolling hills, vibrant tribal culture, festivals, and peaceful villages. It is an offbeat destination for nature lovers and cultural explorers.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Hills, Tribal Culture, Festivals
Major cities: Aizawl, Champhai, Lunglei

Why visit Mizoram?

Mizoram offers breathtaking hills, lush valleys, and a peaceful atmosphere. Experience traditional Mizo festivals, trekking in the hills, exploring waterfalls, and the warm hospitality of local villages.

Top attractions

Aizawl

Champhai

Tam Dil Lake

Food & Experiences

  • Mizo cuisine – Bamboo shoot dishes, smoked meat, rice beer.
  • Trekking in hills and visiting waterfalls.
  • Traditional Mizo festivals like Chapchar Kut.
  • Exploring villages and interacting with locals.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.

Activities: Trekking, village tours, lake visits, cultural experiences.
