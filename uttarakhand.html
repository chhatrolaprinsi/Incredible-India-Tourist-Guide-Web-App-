Uttarakhand – The Land of Gods & Hills

Uttarakhand is a Himalayan state famous for its scenic landscapes, spiritual towns, adventure activities, and national parks. From Rishikesh’s yoga retreats to Nainital’s lakes and Jim Corbett’s wildlife, Uttarakhand is a paradise for nature and culture lovers.

Best time: Mar – Jun & Sep – Nov
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Himalayas, Temples, Adventure
Major cities: Dehradun, Rishikesh, Nainital, Mussoorie

Why visit Uttarakhand?

Uttarakhand offers a mix of spirituality, adventure, and nature. Trekking in the Himalayas, white-water rafting in Rishikesh, lakeside retreats in Nainital, and visits to sacred temples like Haridwar and Badrinath make this state a top destination for travelers seeking peace and adventure.

Top attractions

Rishikesh

Nainital Lake

Jim Corbett National Park

Food & Experiences

  • Local Kumaoni & Garhwali cuisine – Aloo Ke Gutke, Bal Mithai.
  • Yoga, meditation, and river rafting in Rishikesh.
  • Hill station walks – Mussoorie, Nainital, and Almora.
  • Trekking & camping in the Garhwal Himalayas.

Good to know

Best time: March – June & September – November for clear skies and mild weather.

Activities: Trekking, rafting, wildlife spotting, temple visits.
