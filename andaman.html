Andaman & Nicobar Islands – Tropical Paradise

The Andaman & Nicobar Islands are a group of islands in the Bay of Bengal, known for pristine beaches, coral reefs, and adventure activities like scuba diving, snorkeling, and trekking.

Best time: Oct – May
Ideal stay: 4 – 7 days
Famous for: Beaches, Diving, Coral Reefs
Major cities: Port Blair, Havelock, Neil

Why visit Andaman & Nicobar?

These islands are perfect for beach lovers, water sports enthusiasts, and nature admirers. Enjoy turquoise waters, tropical forests, and unique marine life. Relax on Radhanagar Beach, dive in Havelock, and explore the Cellular Jail’s history.

Top attractions

Radhanagar Beach

Havelock Island

Cellular Jail

Food & Experiences

  • Seafood delicacies – Fish curry, Lobster, Crabs.
  • Water sports – Scuba diving, snorkeling, kayaking.
  • Explore tropical forests, caves, and mangroves.
  • Attend local festivals and cultural experiences on the islands.

Good to know

Best time: October – May for beaches and diving.

Activities: Beaches, water sports, trekking, island hopping.
