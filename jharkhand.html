Jharkhand – Land of Waterfalls and Forests

Jharkhand, in eastern India, is known for its lush forests, scenic waterfalls, wildlife sanctuaries, and tribal culture. It’s ideal for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Waterfalls, Forests, Tribal Culture
Major cities: Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Dhanbad

Why visit Jharkhand?

Jharkhand offers stunning natural beauty with waterfalls, hills, and wildlife. Explore Hundru Falls, Dassam Falls, Betla National Park, and enjoy tribal culture and handicrafts.

Top attractions

Hundru Falls

Betla National Park

Dassam Falls

Food & Experiences

  • Traditional Jharkhandi dishes – Dhuska, Thekua, Malpua.
  • Trek through forests and visit waterfalls.
  • Experience tribal culture and handicrafts.
  • Wildlife safaris in national parks.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for sightseeing and trekking.

Activities: Trekking, waterfalls, wildlife safaris, cultural tours.
