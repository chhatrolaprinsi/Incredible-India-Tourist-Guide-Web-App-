Arunachal Pradesh – Land of the Rising Sun

Arunachal Pradesh is a northeastern state known for its lush green valleys, snow-capped mountains, Buddhist monasteries, and vibrant tribal culture. Adventure, spirituality, and nature converge in this serene Himalayan region.

Best time: Mar – Jun & Sep – Nov
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Monasteries, Mountains, Tribal Culture
Major cities: Itanagar, Tawang, Ziro, Pasighat

Why visit Arunachal Pradesh?

Arunachal Pradesh offers breathtaking landscapes, Buddhist monasteries, and tribal villages. Trekking, river rafting, wildlife spotting, and cultural experiences make it an ideal destination for nature lovers and spiritual travelers alike.

Top attractions

Tawang Monastery

Ziro Valley

Sela Pass

Food & Experiences

  • Local tribal cuisine – Thukpa, Momos, Bamboo Shoot dishes.
  • Visit monasteries – Tawang, Bomdila.
  • Adventure activities – trekking, river rafting, birdwatching.
  • Cultural festivals – Ziro Music Festival, Losar Festival.

Good to know

Best time: March – June & September – November for pleasant weather.

Activities: Trekking, monastery visits, cultural tours, wildlife spotting.
