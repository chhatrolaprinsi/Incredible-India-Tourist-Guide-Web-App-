Delhi – The Heart of India

Delhi, the capital city of India, is a vibrant blend of history, culture, and modernity. From Mughal architecture and bustling bazaars to contemporary malls and thriving nightlife, Delhi offers a little bit of everything.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: History, Culture, Food
Airport/Rail: DEL / New Delhi Railway Station

Why visit Delhi?

Delhi is where ancient heritage meets modern India. From grand Mughal forts and temples to bustling street markets and fine dining, it’s a destination that reflects the country’s diversity.

Top attractions

Red Fort

India Gate

Qutub Minar

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather.

Food: Don’t miss Delhi’s street food – Chaat, Parathas at Chandni Chowk, Butter Chicken, and Momos.

Shopping: Dilli Haat, Sarojini Nagar, Chandni Chowk for handicrafts and fashion.

Transport: Delhi Metro is the fastest way to get around the city.
