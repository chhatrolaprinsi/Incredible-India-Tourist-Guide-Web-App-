West Bengal – Culture, Hills, and the Sundarbans

West Bengal in eastern India is known for its cultural heritage, colonial architecture, hill stations, tea gardens, and the unique mangroves of the Sundarbans.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Culture, Hill stations, Sundarbans
Major cities: Kolkata, Darjeeling, Siliguri

Why visit West Bengal?

West Bengal offers a perfect blend of culture, natural beauty, and heritage. Visit Kolkata for colonial architecture and cultural hubs, Darjeeling for tea gardens and Himalayan views, and the Sundarbans for wildlife and mangrove forests.

Top attractions

Darjeeling

Kolkata – Victoria Memorial

Sundarbans National Park

Food & Experiences

  • Bengali cuisine – Fish curry, Mishti Doi, Rosogolla.
  • Explore colonial architecture and art galleries in Kolkata.
  • Ride the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway.
  • Wildlife safari and birdwatching in Sundarbans.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for hill stations and sightseeing.

Activities: Cultural tours, trekking, wildlife safaris, tea garden visits.
