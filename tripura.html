Tripura – Land of Palaces and Lakes

Tripura is a northeastern state known for its royal heritage, ancient temples, scenic lakes, and rich tribal culture. It is ideal for history lovers, nature enthusiasts, and cultural travelers.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 2 – 4 days
Famous for: Palaces, Lakes, Temples
Major cities: Agartala, Udaipur, Dharmanagar

Why visit Tripura?

Tripura is rich in royal heritage and natural beauty. Explore the Ujjayanta Palace, relax by Rudrasagar Lake, visit ancient temples, and experience local tribal culture and festivals.

Top attractions

Ujjayanta Palace

Rudrasagar Lake

Neermahal Palace

Food & Experiences

  • Tripuri cuisine – Bamboo shoot dishes, rice, fish curries.
  • Visit royal palaces and historic temples.
  • Explore lakes, hills, and tribal villages.
  • Attend local festivals and handicraft markets.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Activities: Palace tours, lake visits, trekking, cultural exploration.
