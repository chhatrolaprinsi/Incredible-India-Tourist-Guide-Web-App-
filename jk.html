Jammu & Kashmir – Paradise on Earth

Jammu & Kashmir is famous for its scenic beauty, snow-capped mountains, serene lakes, and rich culture. From the Mughal gardens in Srinagar to pilgrimage sites in Jammu, it’s a perfect destination for nature lovers, adventurers, and spiritual travelers.

Best time: Mar – Oct
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Valleys, Lakes, Mountains
Major cities: Srinagar, Jammu, Leh

Why visit Jammu & Kashmir?

The region offers breathtaking landscapes, adventure activities, and spiritual sites. Srinagar’s Dal Lake, Gulmarg’s skiing slopes, and Vaishno Devi temple in Jammu are major highlights. It’s ideal for nature lovers, photographers, and those seeking peace amidst mountains.

Top attractions

Dal Lake, Srinagar

Gulmarg

Vaishno Devi Temple, Jammu

Food & Experiences

  • Wazwan – traditional Kashmiri feast.
  • Kahwa – green tea with spices.
  • Gushtaba – minced mutton delicacy.
  • Shopping – Pashmina shawls, carpets, handicrafts.

Good to know

Best time: March – October for pleasant weather.

Activities: Trekking, skiing, boating, river rafting.
