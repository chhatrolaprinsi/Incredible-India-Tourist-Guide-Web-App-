Odisha – Land of Temples and Beaches

Odisha, located on the eastern coast of India, is famous for its ancient temples, vibrant culture, beautiful beaches, and rich tribal heritage.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 4 – 6 days
Famous for: Temples, Beaches, Tribal Culture
Major cities: Bhubaneswar, Puri, Konark

Why visit Odisha?

Odisha offers a blend of spiritual heritage, coastal beauty, and cultural richness. Explore the Sun Temple at Konark, Jagannath Temple in Puri, Chilika Lake for birding, and the pristine beaches of Gopalpur.

Top attractions

Konark Sun Temple

Puri Beach & Jagannath Temple

Chilika Lake

Food & Experiences

  • Odisha cuisine – Pakhala Bhata, Dalma, Rasgulla (original recipe).
  • Attend Rath Yatra festival at Puri.
  • Explore tribal villages and handicrafts.
  • Relax at beaches and enjoy bird watching at Chilika Lake.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Activities: Temple visits, beaches, birding, cultural experiences.
