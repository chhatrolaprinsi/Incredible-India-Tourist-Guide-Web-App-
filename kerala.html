Kerala – God’s Own Country

Kerala is famous for its backwaters, hill stations, beaches, and cultural richness. Enjoy houseboat rides, Ayurvedic wellness, wildlife sanctuaries, and vibrant festivals across the state.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 7 – 10 days
Famous for: Backwaters, Hill stations, Beaches, Ayurveda
Major cities: Kochi, Thiruvananthapuram, Munnar, Alappuzha

Why visit Kerala?

Kerala offers a tranquil mix of nature, culture, and adventure. Cruise the scenic backwaters, trek in the Western Ghats, relax on pristine beaches, explore spice plantations, and witness traditional arts like Kathakali and Theyyam.

Top attractions

Munnar Hill Station

Alleppey Backwaters

Kochi Fort & Beaches

Food & Experiences

  • Kerala cuisine – seafood, appam, puttu, Kerala sadya.
  • Houseboat stays on backwaters.
  • Hill station treks and tea plantations in Munnar.
  • Traditional art and festivals – Kathakali, Theyyam.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and festivals.

Activities: Houseboat cruises, trekking, Ayurveda wellness, cultural experiences.
