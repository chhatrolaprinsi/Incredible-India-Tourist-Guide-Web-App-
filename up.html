Uttar Pradesh – Land of Heritage & Spirituality

Uttar Pradesh is India’s most culturally rich state, famous for the Taj Mahal, sacred cities like Varanasi, historical forts, and vibrant festivals. It offers a blend of spirituality, history, and architecture for travelers of all interests.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Monuments, Pilgrimage, Festivals
Major cities: Agra, Varanasi, Lucknow, Mathura

Why visit Uttar Pradesh?

Uttar Pradesh offers a journey through India’s rich history, architecture, and spirituality. Witness the beauty of Mughal monuments, experience rituals along the Ganges in Varanasi, enjoy Awadhi cuisine in Lucknow, and visit Krishna temples in Mathura and Vrindavan.

Top attractions

Taj Mahal, Agra

Varanasi Ghats

Lucknow – Heritage & Cuisine

Food & Experiences

  • Awadhi cuisine – Kebabs, biryani, korma.
  • Street food in Varanasi – Chaat, lassi, malpua.
  • Festivals – Diwali, Holi, Ganga Aarti.
  • Historical tours – Forts, Mughal architecture, temples.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather.

Activities: Sightseeing, boat rides on the Ganges, heritage walks, cultural festivals.
