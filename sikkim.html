Sikkim – The Himalayan Paradise

Sikkim, a Himalayan state, is famous for its snow-capped mountains, Buddhist monasteries, lush valleys, and adventure tourism. It is perfect for trekking, sightseeing, and cultural experiences.

Best time: Mar – Jun, Sep – Nov
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Mountains, Monasteries, Trekking
Major cities: Gangtok, Pelling, Namchi

Why visit Sikkim?

Sikkim offers breathtaking Himalayan scenery, serene monasteries, adventure activities, and rich cultural heritage. Explore mountain valleys, trek to Goecha La, enjoy the vibrant local markets, and savor traditional Sikkimese cuisine.

Top attractions

Tsomgo Lake

Rumtek Monastery

Nathula Pass

Food & Experiences

  • Sikkimese cuisine – Thukpa, Momos, Gundruk Soup.
  • Trekking and mountain adventure activities.
  • Visiting Buddhist monasteries and cultural festivals.
  • Explore scenic valleys and local handicrafts.

Good to know

Best time: March – June and September – November for trekking and sightseeing.

Activities: Trekking, monastery tours, adventure sports, photography.
