Nagaland – Land of Festivals and Hills

Nagaland is a northeastern state known for its vibrant tribal culture, colorful festivals, scenic hills, and rich traditions. It is perfect for adventure, cultural immersion, and offbeat travel experiences.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Hornbill Festival, Hills, Tribes
Major cities: Kohima, Dimapur, Mokokchung

Why visit Nagaland?

Nagaland offers spectacular landscapes, vibrant tribal life, and unique cultural experiences. Attend the Hornbill Festival, explore hill towns, hike through forests, and enjoy the traditional crafts and cuisine of the Naga tribes.

Top attractions

Hornbill Festival

Kohima

Dzükou Valley

Food & Experiences

  • Naga cuisine – Smoked meats, bamboo shoot dishes, rice beer.
  • Attend tribal festivals and cultural performances.
  • Trekking and exploring valleys and hills.
  • Village visits and craft shopping.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for festivals and pleasant weather.

Activities: Trekking, festival visits, cultural experiences, photography.
