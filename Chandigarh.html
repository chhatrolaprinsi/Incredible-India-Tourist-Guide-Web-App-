Chandigarh – The City Beautiful

Chandigarh is India’s first planned city—clean, green and refreshingly organized. It’s a perfect base for exploring the Shivalik foothills while enjoying lakes, rock gardens, and a vibrant café scene.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 2 – 3 days
Nearest hills: Kasauli, Shimla
Airport/Rail: IXC / CDG Jn.

Why visit Chandigarh?

Chandigarh blends modernist architecture with leafy boulevards... while the city’s grid layout makes navigation effortless.
