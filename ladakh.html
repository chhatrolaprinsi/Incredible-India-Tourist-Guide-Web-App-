Ladakh – Land of High Passes

Ladakh, the cold desert of India, is famous for its stark beauty, high mountains, Buddhist monasteries, and adventurous activities. From Pangong Lake to Nubra Valley, Ladakh is a paradise for trekkers, bikers, and photographers.

Best time: May – September
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Mountains, Lakes, Adventure
Major places: Leh, Nubra Valley, Pangong Lake

Why visit Ladakh?

Ladakh is unique for its high-altitude deserts, crystal-clear lakes, and Buddhist culture. It offers trekking routes, motorbike adventures, river rafting, and serene monasteries. The region is perfect for travelers seeking solitude and adventure in nature.

Top attractions

Pangong Lake

Nubra Valley

Monasteries of Leh

Food & Experiences

  • Thukpa – Tibetan-style noodle soup.
  • Momos – Steamed or fried dumplings.
  • Butter Tea – Traditional salty tea.
  • Shopping – Pashmina shawls, Ladakhi handicrafts, jewelry.

Good to know

Best time: May – September for accessible roads and mild weather.

Activities: Trekking, biking, river rafting, monastery visits.
