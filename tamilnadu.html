Tamil Nadu – Temples, Beaches & Hill Stations

Tamil Nadu is famous for its Dravidian temples, scenic beaches, vibrant culture, and hill stations. Explore Chennai, Madurai, Ooty, Kodaikanal, and coastal towns along the Bay of Bengal.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 7 – 10 days
Famous for: Temples, Beaches, Hill Stations, Culture
Major cities: Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Ooty

Why visit Tamil Nadu?

Tamil Nadu blends rich history, spiritual experiences, and natural beauty. From majestic Dravidian temples to scenic hill stations and serene beaches, the state offers diverse experiences for culture seekers, nature lovers, and adventure enthusiasts.

Top attractions

Meenakshi Temple, Madurai

Marina Beach, Chennai

Ooty Hill Station

Food & Experiences

  • South Indian cuisine – idli, dosa, sambar, filter coffee.
  • Temple festivals, cultural performances, and traditional arts.
  • Beach walks, hill station treks, and tea plantation visits.
  • Shopping in local markets for handicrafts and textiles.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather.

Activities: Temple tours, beach visits, hill station treks, cultural exploration.
