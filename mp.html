Madhya Pradesh – Heart of India with Heritage & Nature

Madhya Pradesh is centrally located and known for its historical forts, UNESCO heritage sites, national parks, and rich tribal culture. It’s perfect for history buffs and nature lovers alike.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 5 – 7 days
Famous for: Heritage, National Parks, Temples
Major cities: Bhopal, Gwalior, Khajuraho, Jabalpur

Why visit Madhya Pradesh?

Madhya Pradesh offers rich history, UNESCO heritage sites like Khajuraho temples, majestic forts in Gwalior and Mandu, and abundant wildlife at Kanha and Bandhavgarh National Parks.

Top attractions

Kanha National Park

Khajuraho Temples

Gwalior Fort

Food & Experiences

  • Madhya Pradesh cuisine – Poha, Bhutte ka Kees, Dal Bafla.
  • Explore tribal villages and traditional arts.
  • Wildlife safaris in Kanha and Bandhavgarh.
  • Historical tours in Gwalior, Mandu, and Orchha.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for sightseeing and wildlife.

Activities: Wildlife safaris, heritage tours, temple visits, cultural experiences.
