Maharashtra – Culture, Mountains, and Coast

Maharashtra is a diverse state with vibrant cities, historic forts, hill stations, and beaches. Mumbai offers cosmopolitan life, while hill stations like Lonavala and Mahabaleshwar provide scenic retreats.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 7 – 10 days
Famous for: Mumbai, Hill stations, Beaches, Heritage
Major cities: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik

Why visit Maharashtra?

Maharashtra offers a perfect mix of urban culture, nature, and history. Explore Mumbai’s iconic landmarks, enjoy hill station treks, relax at coastal beaches, and visit ancient forts and UNESCO heritage sites.

Top attractions

Gateway of India, Mumbai

Ajanta & Ellora Caves

Lonavala & Hill Stations

Food & Experiences

  • Maharashtrian cuisine – vada pav, pav bhaji, poha, puran poli.
  • Beaches – Alibaug, Tarkarli, Ganpatipule.
  • Hill station treks – Lonavala, Matheran, Mahabaleshwar.
  • Cultural experiences – Ganesh Chaturthi festivals in Mumbai and Pune.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and festivals.

Activities: City sightseeing, heritage tours, beach relaxation, hill station trekking.
