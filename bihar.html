Bihar – Land of History and Culture

Bihar, an eastern Indian state, is famous for its ancient heritage, Buddhist pilgrimage sites, historical monuments, and riverside landscapes along the Ganges.

Best time: Oct – Mar
Ideal stay: 3 – 5 days
Famous for: Buddhist sites, Monuments, Culture
Major cities: Patna, Bodh Gaya, Nalanda

Why visit Bihar?

Bihar is rich in historical and religious significance. Explore Bodh Gaya, where Buddha attained enlightenment, visit ancient Nalanda University ruins, and see the architectural marvels of Patna.

Top attractions

Bodh Gaya

Nalanda University Ruins

Patna Sahib Gurudwara

Food & Experiences

  • Bihari cuisine – Litti Chokha, Sattu Paratha, Fish curry.
  • Visit historical and religious sites.
  • Explore riverside Ghats and cultural festivals.
  • Experience local handicrafts and folk music.

Good to know

Best time: October – March for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Activities: Pilgrimage tours, history exploration, cultural experiences.
