Meghalaya – Abode of Clouds

Meghalaya, the “Abode of Clouds,” is known for its lush hills, living root bridges, waterfalls, and rich tribal culture. This northeastern state is perfect for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts alike.

Best time: Oct – May
Ideal stay: 4 – 6 days
Famous for: Living Root Bridges, Waterfalls, Caves
Major cities: Shillong, Cherrapunji, Mawlynnong

Why visit Meghalaya?

Meghalaya is a paradise of misty hills, unique living root bridges, and cascading waterfalls. Experience tribal festivals, explore caves, trek through lush forests, and enjoy the serene hill stations.

Top attractions

Living Root Bridges

Food & Experiences

  • Local Khasi cuisine – Jadoh, Dohneiiong, Tungrymbai.
  • Trekking and photography in hills and forests.
  • Visit waterfalls like Nohkalikai and Seven Sisters.
  • Explore living root bridges and tribal villages.

Good to know

Best time: October – May for trekking, sightseeing, and pleasant weather.

Activities: Trekking, waterfall visits, village tours, photography.
